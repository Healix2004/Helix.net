# Helix UI/UX Data Requirements

This document outlines the core screens across the Helix healthcare platform and the specific data payloads required from the backend APIs to render the UI components.

---

## 1. Common / Authentication Flow

### 1.1. Login & Registration
* **Description:** The gateway to the platform. Users select their role (Patient, Doctor, Pharmacy/Lab) and authenticate.
* **Data Needed:**
    * `Email` and `Password` (for Login).
    * `Role` selection (Enum: Patient, Doctor, Pharmacy).
    * **Response:** JWT Bearer Token, User ID, Role, and Profile Completion Status.

### 1.2. Onboarding Wizards
* **Description:** Multi-step forms for users to complete their profiles after initial signup.
* **Patient Data Needed:** Full Name, Gender, National ID, DOB, Phone, Blood Type, Chronic Diseases, Allergies, Emergency Contact, Insurance Provider/ID.
* **Doctor Data Needed:** Profile Photo, Specialty, Subspecialty, Clinic Name, Address, License Number, Years of Experience, Available Days/Times, Consultation Fees, Document Uploads (License, ID).
* **Pharmacy Data Needed:** Pharmacy Name, License Number, Address, Registration Year, Document Uploads.

## 2. Doctor Portal

### 2.1. Doctor Dashboard
* **Description:** The landing page for doctors showing daily metrics, AI insights, and upcoming appointments.
* **Data Needed:**
    * **Header:** Doctor Name, Initials, Specialty.
    * **KPI Metrics:** Today's Appointments (Count & Trend %), Critical Alerts (Count), Pending Labs, Active Patients.
    * **Charts:** Appointment Trends (Scheduled vs Completed per month).
    * **AI Assistant Alerts:** Categorized lists (High/Medium/Low priority) like "3 patients have abnormal results" or "5 patients due for follow-up".
    * **Today's Appointments:** Array of objects containing: `PatientName`, `Initials`, `AppointmentType` (e.g., Cardiology Checkup), `Time`, `Status` (Upcoming, Waiting, Completed, Urgent).

### 2.2. Patient Profile & Timeline (Accessed via QR)
* **Description:** A detailed view of a patient's medical history, unlocked temporarily via scanning a QR code.
* **Data Needed:**
    * **Patient Header:** Name, ID, Age, Gender, Active Status, Phone, Email, Insurance Plan.
    * **Clinical Summary (Right Sidebar):** * `KnownAllergies` (List of strings)
        * `ChronicConditions` (List of strings)
        * `CurrentMedications` (List of Active Drugs + Adherence percentage).
    * **Last Visit Summary:** Date, Reason, Vitals (BP, HR, Temp, O2), Doctor Notes.
    * **Medical Timeline:** Chronological array of clinical events (Vaccinations, Lab Results, Physical Exams) including dates, providing doctors, and result details.

### 2.3. New Prescription Screen
* **Description:** The screen where doctors prescribe new medications.
* **Data Needed:**
    * **Search Catalog:** Dropdown populated from the `MedicalConcept` database (Drug name, dosage forms).
    * **Prescription Payload (To Send):** `MedicationId`, `Dosage` (e.g., 500mg), `Frequency` (e.g., Twice daily), `Duration` (e.g., 7 days).

### 2.4. AI Drug Interaction Alert
* **Description:** A modal/overlay that pops up if the doctor prescribes a drug that interacts negatively with the patient's existing medications.
* **Data Needed:**
    * `DrugA` (New prescription)
    * `DrugB` (Existing medication)
    * `RiskLevel` (e.g., "85% High Risk")
    * `Description` (e.g., "Increases risk of severe bleeding")
    * `AIRecommendation` (Alternative safer drug suggestions).

## 3. Patient Portal

### 3.1. Patient Dashboard
* **Description:** A summary of the patient's health status, upcoming tasks, and AI health tips.
* **Data Needed:**
    * **KPIs:** Total Records, Active Prescriptions, Upcoming Appointments.
    * **Recent Activity:** A timeline feed of recent events (e.g., "Lab Results Available 2 hours ago").
    * **Health Score:** An AI-generated metric (e.g., 85/100).
    * **Health Tips:** AI-generated personalized advice (e.g., "Stay Hydrated").

### 3.2. Medical Records (Labs, Radiology, Documents)
* **Description:** Tabbed lists showing all historical medical data.
* **Data Needed (List View):** `TestName`/`ScanType`, `Date`, `RequestedBy`, `Status` (Pending, Completed, Abnormal).
* **Data Needed (Detail View):** PDF file URLs, Radiologist/Doctor notes, Tabular data for bloodwork (Parameter, Result, Normal Range, High/Low flag).

### 3.3. Patient Access QR Code
* **Description:** A dynamic QR code generated by the patient to grant temporary access to a doctor or pharmacy.
* **Data Needed:** `PatientName`, `PatientId`, `TemporaryAccessCode` (e.g., MED-8X067AJK), `ExpirationTime`.

## 4. Pharmacy Portal

### 4.1. Pharmacy Dashboard
* **Description:** Operational overview for pharmacists.
* **Data Needed:**
    * **KPIs:** Prescriptions Dispensed Today, Pending Orders, Critical Alerts, Revenue Today.
    * **Today's Prescriptions List:** `PatientName`, `PrescriptionID`, `Department`, `Time`, `Status` (Active, Pending, Dispensed).
    * **Stock Alerts:** Inventory warnings (e.g., "Metformin 500mg - Critical restock").

### 4.2. QR Scanner & Dispensing View
* **Description:** The pharmacist scans the patient's QR code to view and dispense a prescription.
* **Data Needed:**
    * **Patient Demographics & Allergies:** To ensure safety before dispensing.
    * **Prescription Details:** Array of medications to dispense (`DrugName`, `Dosage`, `Frequency`, `Duration`, `DoctorInstructions`).
    * **Prescribing Doctor Info:** Name, Specialty, License Number.

---

## 5. Global AI Assistant (Chatbot)

### 5.1. Chat Interface
* **Description:** A floating or dedicated chat window where users can ask medical questions based on their data.
* **Data Needed:**
    * **Patient Context (Invisible to user, sent to AI):** Patient Age, Gender, Chronic Conditions, Current Medications.
    * **Chat History:** Array of previous messages (`Role`, `Content`).
    * **Suggested Prompts:** Quick-click buttons (e.g., "Explain my lab results", "Show recent prescriptions").
